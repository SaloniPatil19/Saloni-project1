Famous Temples in Maharashtra

Grishneshwar Temple

Grishneshwar Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlinga temples of Lord Shiva. It is located near Ellora in Maharashtra.

Trimbakeshwar Temple

Trimbakeshwar Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas and is located near Nashik.

Bhimashankar Temple

Bhimashankar is a famous Jyotirlinga temple located in the Sahyadri hills of Maharashtra.

Tulja Bhavani Temple

Tulja Bhavani Temple is a famous temple dedicated to Goddess Bhavani in Tuljapur.

Siddhivinayak Temple

Siddhivinayak Temple is a famous temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha located in Mumbai.

Shirdi Sai Baba Temple

Shirdi is a famous pilgrimage destination associated with Shri Sai Baba and attracts devotees from many places.

About Temples in Maharashtra

Maharashtra is famous for its rich cultural, historical and spiritual heritage. The state has many ancient temples that attract devotees and tourists throughout the year.

These temples represent the culture, traditions, architecture and religious heritage of Maharashtra.
